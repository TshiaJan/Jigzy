# Privacy Policy for Jigzy: Jigsaw Puzzle Grid

**Last Updated:** September 17, 2026

Welcome to **Jigzy** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe, enjoyable gaming experience. This Privacy Policy explains how our game handles player information.

---

### 1. Information We Do Not Collect
- **No Personal Registration:** Jigzy does not ask for personal information, names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account logins.
- **No Device Sensors or Contacts Access:** The app does not access your camera, microphone, photo gallery, location coordinates, or contacts list.

---

### 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
To track your puzzle journey, Jigzy saves game state locally on your device via `localStorage`:
- **Level Progress & Statistics:** Your current level, best level unlocked, total puzzles solved, and loss count.
- **Tokens Balance:** Fictional in-game tokens earned by solving jigsaw levels.
- **Player Settings:** Audio settings (sound effects toggle), placement guide highlights, tap/drag controls, and easy mode preference.

*All game data remains strictly inside your local device storage. Clearing application data or uninstalling the app will reset your progress.*

---

### 3. Third-Party Services & Optional Advertisements
Jigzy is designed to be fully enjoyable offline. In commercial app store distributions:
- **Optional Rewarded Ads:** Players may choose to watch an optional video advertisement to earn bonus tokens or retry attempts.
- **Standard Advertising Identifiers:** Third-party advertising networks (e.g., Google AdMob) may collect anonymous, non-personal identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or IDFA) to deliver advertisements in compliance with privacy regulations.
- You can reset or opt out of personalized ad tracking via your device settings:
  - *Android:* Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID
  - *iOS:* Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
Jigzy is suitable for all age groups and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR. We do not collect or solicit personal data from children under 13.
